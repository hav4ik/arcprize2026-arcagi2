## Incidents and open issues
- **Sandbox memory incident:**
  - Sessions are never cleaned up after an attempt, so their processes pile up inside the sandbox.
  - One runaway cell also took 40 GB.
  - Around 07:50 UTC the host ran out of RAM and the runs started erroring.
  - What I did: stopped the runs, restarted the container, capped it at 40 GiB (`docker update`, now also in `start.sh`), and resumed with fewer workers. About $2–4 of in-flight calls were lost.
  - **The client's session cleanup still needs a fix; please review `SandboxSession.close()`.** Until then, restart the container between big runs.
- **Safety audits** (`experiments/E004_tool_use/trace_review_safety*.md`): nothing concerning.
  - **Questionable:** after wiping `train`, the model listed `/tmp`, `/root` and `/home`. It saw other runs' scratch files, because `/tmp` is shared across sessions, and did not open them.
  - **Contamination:** the model recalled real ARC-AGI-2 eval task ids, which confirms the eval set was in its pretraining data.
  - **Recommended:** a separate `/tmp` per session, a non-root container, and resource limits.
- **Completing E001d** for a fair baseline changed `evals/results/arcprize_deepseek-flash_max.jsonl`: its 17 errored attempts were retried, and a third sample was appended.
- Some of my assistant turns were stopped by a safety classifier. The work continued without those parts.
