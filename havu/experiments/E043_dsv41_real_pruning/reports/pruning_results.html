Routing is spread over many experts

Effective number of experts per layer, exp(entropy of routing frequency): 83-196 of 384. Keeping 48-72 experts therefore cuts into experts that carry real traffic in every layer.

Tool-call experts are a partly separate set

Per layer, the share of the 72 most important experts for tool-call tokens that are also in the 72 most important overall: 42-64% in early layers. This is why general importance scores dropped the tool-call format.

Findings

  • Engram rows: a 64 GiB top-frequency table (34% of rows) is lossless on ARC (control 69.5% vs full 65.9%).
  • Expert pruning to E72/E64/E48 costs most of the accuracy even after fixing the tool-call format: routing is spread over 83-196 effective experts per layer, and the kept sets cover 53-65% of the routing traffic.
  • E72 mostly keeps the puzzle's main idea and loses on execution (near-miss answers, 45% tool-call errors); E64 has semantic damage (hallucinated module names); E48 never leaves its first turn.
  • Teacher-forced NLL understates the damage: the format collapse and the looping only show up in free generation.
