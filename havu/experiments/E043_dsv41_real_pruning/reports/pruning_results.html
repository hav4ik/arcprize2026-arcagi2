Findings

  • Engram rows: a 64 GiB top-frequency table (34% of rows) is within NLL noise of the full table and generates normally; misses come from novel n-grams in the model's own reasoning (93% of unseen lookups).
  • All-token NLL and contribution-based keep sets hide a collapse of the DSML tool-call format at E72 and below (0/10 valid first-turn calls); weighting tool-call-block tokens x30 restores 10/10 at E64/E72.
  • Trace reviews: E64 also has semantic damage (hallucinated module names, 57% of tool calls error vs 1.7% for the full model); E72 mostly keeps the puzzle's main idea and loses on execution (near-miss answers).
  • E96 and above generate cleanly but need ~21 GiB/GPU of weights, above the 4xL4 budget; E72 is the ceiling.
